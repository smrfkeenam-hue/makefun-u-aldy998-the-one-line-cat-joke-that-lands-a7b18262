# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition `u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262`
("The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands", $2 winner-takes-all).

- `main.py` (stdlib only) writes one original cat joke, one line under 200
  characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.
- The joke: a cat pushes a glass off the counter, watches it shatter, then
  blames the human with a look — specific cat behaviour with a blame-reversal
  twist at the end, no setup needed, no tired cat pun.
- Judged blind, pairwise, by z-ai/glm-5.3-flash; winner takes the whole $2.
